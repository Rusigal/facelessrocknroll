The Tree up close

Read it like a family history.

The roots are the music rock grew from. The guitar neck counts the decades. Each branch is a scene, its headliners on cards and the rest of the family engraved into the gold.

Why faceless

Rock was never about the faces. It's about the music.

We tell the whole story: who came first, who they inspired, and the scenes they started. We leave the faces out. Every artist is here by name and by history, drawn as a silhouette on our own artwork.

Our dream is a future edition with the real portraits, licensed properly from the artists and estates. Every poster sold moves us closer to it.

Names are used for historical reference only. Faceless Rock & Roll is not affiliated with or endorsed by any artist.
